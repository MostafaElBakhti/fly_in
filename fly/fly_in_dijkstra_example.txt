Current Dijkstra Rules
----------------------
- Blocked zones are excluded.
- Entering a restricted zone costs 2.
- Entering any other usable zone costs 1.
- Entering a priority zone adds +1 to priority_score.
- The next current zone is chosen using:
    (distance, -priority_score, zone.name)
- On equal distance, the route with the higher priority_score is preferred.
- Already visited nodes are skipped with:
    if neighbor not in unvisited:
        continue
